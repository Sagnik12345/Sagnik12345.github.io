Gamma–Gamma Coincidence

Micro Project · Prof. Satyabrata Raj, IISER Kolkata

γ–γ coincidence experiments with 60Co and 137Cs sources using NaI(Tl) (2″×2″, 3″×3″), CdZnTe and HPGe detectors. MCA spectra, peak calibration, FWHM and energy resolution; Compton background subtraction to isolate coincidence peaks; resolution vs. efficiency comparison with tuned HV and amplifier settings.

NuclearPythonOriginDetectors
